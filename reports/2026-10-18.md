# Research review — 2026-10-18

The English index now contains **52 curated papers**, including seven additions. Four were first submitted after October 9, EndoRSI shares the previous review date, and RoboRSI and PhysEvo fill earlier coverage gaps. Dates below refer to initial arXiv submission; a revision is not counted as another paper.

## Additions

- **CarryOn** — October 15, v1. Couples persistent preferences and scene context with action shielding and recovery skills around a frozen VLA. Its recovery measure accumulates successful completion across reflection rounds and retries; it should not be presented as single-attempt recovery accuracy. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21453v1), [project](https://vincent2311.github.io/Projects/CarryOn/), [code](https://github.com/JIA-Lab-research/CarryOn).
- **Temporal Flow Policy** — October 14, v1. Models learned memory transitions over physical time and conditions robot policies on that state. Included for its direct relevance to memory mechanisms: this involves policy training, unlike a frozen-model runtime wrapper. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20524v1).
- **EmbodiedEvo** — October 13, v2 revised October 14. Turns execution feedback into reusable code skills and diagnostic lessons, then freezes the experience for agents in unseen scenes. The experience is collected per task; unseen-scene evaluation does not establish unseen-task or real-robot transfer. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18867v2).
- **RoboSimStudio** — October 13, v2 revised October 14. Gives humans and coding agents access to physical parameters, contact models, and solver choices when constructing and refining simulations. Visual resemblance alone does not verify contact dynamics or real-world transfer; track tuning effort and physical feedback. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18835v2), [project](https://robosimstudio.github.io/).
- **EndoRSI** — October 9, v1. Uses physical failure evidence to revise endoscopic robot policies, with execution isolated in a constrained runtime under human authorization. The reported laboratory and ex-vivo evidence does not establish clinical deployment or human-patient safety. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13376v1).
- **RoboRSI** — October 8, v1; backfill. Assigns failures to a skill hierarchy, limits repairs to the responsible capabilities, and validates reusable releases with agent roles and human steering. Human supervision and repeated attempts remain relevant to the reported task pass rates. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12424v1), [project](https://lab.noematrix.ai/blog/2-roborsi/), [code](https://github.com/nssmd/RoboRSI).
- **PhysEvo** — October 6, v1; backfill. Revises control tools, manipulation skills, and diagnostic tools from execution trajectories around a frozen model. Held-out layouts use retained task-specific versions; the real-world study includes continued revision and 25 trials across five tasks. This is not evidence of zero-shot sim-to-real transfer. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08995v1).

## Implications for evaluation

These are maintainer interpretations, not new experimental findings:

1. **Declare what persists.** Separate model weights, task-specific skills, diagnostic lessons, user preferences, and scene state. Report whether each component is frozen or updated during evaluation.
2. **Declare the attempt budget.** Compare first-attempt success and cumulative completion separately, alongside resets, retries, human interventions, and elapsed time. A larger recovery budget can change the meaning of a headline score.
3. **Separate construction from transfer.** Simulation building, calibration, held-out simulation evaluation, and physical deployment need distinct evidence. Likewise, scene generalization and task generalization require different splits.

## Verification and coverage limits

New entries were checked against primary arXiv abstracts and submission histories, supplemented by the linked author project pages and code repositories. Their verification level remains `primary_abstract`; this review does not claim full-text reproduction, code execution, independent experimental validation, or exhaustive literature coverage. Numeric metric fields remain empty pending structured verification of experimental conditions.

Discovery used targeted searches for robot harnesses, memory, recovery, self-improvement, and simulation. Search indexing and API availability can leave gaps. Official project/code links were added only where checked. Repository tests validate structure and local links; they do not validate scientific results or prove that scheduled GitHub Actions have run.
