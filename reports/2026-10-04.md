## Verification scope

Checked the arXiv v1 abstract and the author project page, which links the official code. Categories: `execution` (the runtime agent selects and composes policies through interfaces that state each prior and its applicability), `skills` (a construction agent builds a library of prior-specific policies from demonstrations), and `policy-learning` (verification results on demonstrated entry states feed back into proposing an additional prior per skill).

No metrics were recorded. The paper reports results only in simulation (MetaWorld and ManiSkill), and its settings differ by experiment, so the numbers are not comparable across entries without the matching protocol.
