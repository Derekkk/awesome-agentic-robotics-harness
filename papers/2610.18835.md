RoboSimStudio: An Interactive Toolkit for Human and Agent Rapid Real2Sim of Robot Manipulation Tasks

## Mechanism overview

Exposes solver choices, contact models, and physical parameters for humans and coding agents to refine simulations against observed behavior, supporting agentic real2sim2real experiments.

## Limitations and research questions

Building a visually plausible simulator is not sufficient evidence of correct contact dynamics. Account for human tuning, force feedback, construction time, and separate real-world transfer tests.
