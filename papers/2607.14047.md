# Zero2Skill

Zero2Skill: Bootstrapping Robot Skills through Autonomous Data Collection, Training, and Deployment

- [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14047)

## Mechanism overview

Collects data, verifies outcomes, and resets autonomously; requests human help only after exhausting a retry budget, then parses language corrections into Corrective Memory for reuse across rounds.

## Limitations and research questions

Reduced human effort does not necessarily mean reduced elapsed time; distinguish collection success, verification accuracy, and trained-policy performance.
