# EndoRSI

EndoRSI: Physical-Evidence-Driven Recursive Improvement of Endoscopic Robot Policies

- First submitted: 2026-10-09; reviewed version: v1; latest revision: no additional revision recorded

- [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13376)

## Mechanism overview

Uses failed physical experiments to revise endoscopic policies while isolating robot execution in a constrained runtime under human authorization.

## Limitations and research questions

The abstract reports laboratory and ex-vivo porcine evidence, not clinical deployment or human-patient safety. Include human oversight and distinguish generation-specific adaptation from frozen-policy evaluation.
