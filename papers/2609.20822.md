## Mechanism overview

Turns obstacle-avoidance requirements into action choices during routing and contact, combining waypoint validation with replanning.

## Limitations and research questions

Report task success and collision avoidance separately; neither constitutes a formal safety guarantee. The v2 title is Coding Agents with Harness for Safe Robot Control; the earlier title referred to an obstacle-aware harness for manipulation.
