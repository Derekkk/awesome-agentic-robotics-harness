## Mechanism overview

Assigns outcomes to branches of a skill hierarchy, confines repairs to responsible capabilities, and validates reusable skill releases with coordinated agent roles and human steering.

## Limitations and research questions

Human supervision remains part of real-world deployment. The project distinguishes cumulative task pass rates from per-episode success; account for repeated attempts and parallel execution.
