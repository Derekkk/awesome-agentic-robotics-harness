## Mechanism overview

Evolves code critics and recovery skills around a frozen base policy, separating the timescales of actions, rollouts, and update validation.

## Limitations and research questions

High scores depend on the rollout budget used in the paper; action-rate governance does not imply large-model inference at the action rate.
