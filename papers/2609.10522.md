Show-Harness: Just a VLM Agent Can Play Robots

## Mechanism overview

A VLM emits discrete semantic action units that an embodiment-specific interpreter deterministically converts into local actions, with GUI support for collecting demonstrations.

## Limitations and research questions

Record zero-shot frontier-model configurations separately from fine-tuned smaller-model configurations.
