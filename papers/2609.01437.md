# HarnessDev

HarnessDev: Can LLMs Create and Evolve Their Own Agent Harness?

- First submitted: 2026-09-01; reviewed version: v1; latest revision: no additional revision recorded
- Reviewed on: 2026-09-22; verification scope: `primary_abstract`
- Scope: general-purpose agents; robotics transfer remains unverified
- Categories: evaluation, transfer
- Interface / mechanism: harness generation
- [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01437)

## Mechanism overview

Evaluates the capability and cost of building and improving executable harnesses, including held-out tasks and transfer across models.

## Limitations and research questions

The evidence concerns general-purpose software agents; transfer to physical robots still requires experiments.
