## Mechanism overview

Alternates policy optimization with human-guided judge calibration, using diagnosed failures to revise the training curriculum and verification feedback.

## Limitations and research questions

Human calibration is part of the learning budget. Driving and navigation evidence does not establish manipulation performance; judge improvement needs independent evaluation.
