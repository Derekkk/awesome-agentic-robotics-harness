# RoCo

RoCo: Dialectic Multi-Robot Collaboration with Large Language Models

## Mechanism overview

Multiple robots use dialogue to produce task decompositions and waypoints, then check feasibility using environment feedback and motion planning.

## Limitations and research questions

Record multi-robot collaboration separately from shared learning across robots.
