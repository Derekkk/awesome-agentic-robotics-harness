# StreamVLA

StreamVLA: Breaking the Reason-Act Cycle via Completion-State Gating

## Mechanism overview

Generates textual and visual completion states at subtask transitions, while locking intent and continuously producing actions during stable execution.

## Limitations and research questions

Check whether both normal progress and execution failures reliably trigger replanning.
