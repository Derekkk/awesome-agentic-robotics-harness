## Mechanism overview

A context compiler retains task-relevant visual transitions; a VLM proposes robot tool actions that a constrained controller checks and executes.

## Limitations and research questions

Context adaptation is not parameter learning. The project reports three real-robot trials per condition and observed inter-arm collisions; distinguish action-labeled context from human video and avoid broad reliability or safety claims.
