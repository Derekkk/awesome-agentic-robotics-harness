## Mechanism overview

Combines contrasting execution memories, failure attribution, and tool-based interventions around frozen VLAs, consolidating traces offline for future in-context adaptation.

## Limitations and research questions

Distinct from RoboHarness arXiv:2607.18060. Frozen policy weights do not remove perception, intervention-tool, or memory-construction costs; compare matched backbone and disturbance settings.
