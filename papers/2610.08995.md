## Mechanism overview

Uses execution trajectories to revise control tools, manipulation skills, and diagnostic tools around a frozen model, retaining tested changes for subsequent tasks and improvement.

## Limitations and research questions

Held-out layouts use retained task-specific versions. Real-world results include continued skill revision and 25 trials across five tasks; they are not zero-shot sim-to-real evidence or directly comparable to different RoboDojo protocols.
