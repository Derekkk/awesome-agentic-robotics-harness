## Mechanism overview

Exposes depth, persistent visual anchors, geometry measurements, and grasp proposals as tools; collected tool-call traces also support training smaller robot-use agents.

## Limitations and research questions

Appendix C.3 uses 18 matched cases for the frontier-model comparison and changes several interface components together. Distinguish frozen-model execution from student training and from published baselines with different protocols.
