# MALMM

MALMM: Multi-Agent Large Language Models for Zero-Shot Robotics Manipulation

- [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.17636)

## Mechanism overview

Assigns task planning, control-code generation, and process supervision to separate agents, using stepwise feedback to handle failures.

## Limitations and research questions

This is a division of labor among agents within one system; it does not automatically imply collaboration among multiple robots.
