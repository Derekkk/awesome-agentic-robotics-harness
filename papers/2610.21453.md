## Mechanism overview

Combines preference-aware planning with runtime action shielding and reusable recovery skills around a frozen VLA; interaction updates context and reflection refines recovery.

## Limitations and research questions

The project reports cumulative completion across reflection rounds and retries, not single-attempt recovery accuracy. Separate personalization, violation rate, and recovery; reduced violations are not a formal safety guarantee.
