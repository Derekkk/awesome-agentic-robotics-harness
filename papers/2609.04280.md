# EvoHarnessBench

EvoHarnessBench: Can Your Agents Keep Pace with an Evolving Harness?

- First submitted: 2026-09-03; reviewed version: v2; latest revision: 2026-09-10
- Reviewed on: 2026-09-22; verification scope: `primary_abstract`
- Scope: general-purpose agents; robotics transfer remains unverified
- Categories: evaluation, transfer
- Interface / mechanism: evolving tools/skills/agents
- [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04280)

## Mechanism overview

Expands an external harness in stages and separately evaluates retention of existing capabilities and adaptation to new ones.

## Limitations and research questions

Forgetting in general-purpose agents motivates robotics hypotheses; it is not a robotics result.
