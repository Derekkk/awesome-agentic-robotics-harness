## Mechanism overview

Maintains an episode in the native MLLM context and asynchronously guides an action model through cognition tokens and token age.

## Limitations and research questions

A jointly trained interface is not a plug-and-play frozen VLA; distinguish refresh, inference, and playback frequencies.
