# TwinRL

TwinRL: Digital Twin-Driven Reinforcement Learning for Real-World Robotic Manipulation

- First submitted: 2026-02-09; reviewed version: v4; latest revision: 2026-05-19

## Mechanism overview

Reduces online learning cost through SFT support-set expansion, digital-twin RL warm-up, and targeted real-world exploration.

## Limitations and research questions

Include digital-twin construction in total cost instead of comparing only minutes of real-world interaction.
