## Mechanism overview

Checks navigation proposals against evidence and geometry, using event memory and a persistent spatial graph to guide execution, recovery, and termination.

## Limitations and research questions

Training-free refers to task adaptation, not the absence of pretrained perception or control. Navigation success does not establish contact-manipulation capability.
