# REMAC

REMAC: Self-Reflective and Self-Evolving Multi-Agent Collaboration for Long-Horizon Robot Manipulation

## Mechanism overview

Combines precondition and postcondition checks, self-reflection, and dynamic planning for long-horizon multi-robot tasks.

## Limitations and research questions

Self-evolving plans do not imply updates to policy weights.
