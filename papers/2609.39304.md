## Mechanism overview

Optimizes a visual robot-control harness using rollout batches and accepts a revision only when it beats the incumbent on fixed evaluation cases.

## Limitations and research questions

Section 4 separates held-out tabletop tasks from RoboCasa optimization cases, which have no held-out set. Experiments are simulated; larger batches also consume more rollouts.
