## Mechanism overview

Models harness costs through bounded context, persistent memory, model calls, and imperfect verification, deriving resource tradeoffs and checkpointing predictions.

## Limitations and research questions

Theory and language-agent experiments provide a comparison framework, not robot validation. Physical latency, resets, and sensor errors require additional modeling.
