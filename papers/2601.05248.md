LaST$_{0}$: Latent Spatio-Temporal Chain-of-Thought for Robotic Vision-Language-Action Model

## Mechanism overview

Connects a low-frequency reasoning expert to a high-frequency action expert through latent reasoning that incorporates visual dynamics, 3D structure, and proprioception.

## Limitations and research questions

Compare latent-reasoning benefits against baselines with matched parameter counts and compute budgets.
